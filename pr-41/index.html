Graybox Platformer

A minimal 3D platformer scaffold in Godot 4. Gray capsules, real pipeline.

Your browser does not support the canvas tag.

How to play

  • WASD or ←↑↓→ to move
  • Space to jump

Goal

Collect the 6 coins scattered across the platforms, then touch the finish flag to win. Fall off the edge and you’ll respawn back at the start.

About

Built with Godot 4 and shipped through a real pipeline: automated web builds, scripted playtests with video capture, and versioned releases.
